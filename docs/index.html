Photocircuits

Solve DC circuits from a photo or a hand-drawn sketch, step by step.

Point the camera at a schematic, or draw one on the dot grid. Photocircuits recognises the circuit, lets you check it, then walks through nodal, mesh and series/parallel solutions with the equations written out.
